# Image provenance and reuse

Product photographs are downloaded unchanged from their documented sources and credited to their actual photographers or uploaders. Each image's own license is linked beside the displayed photograph. The exact source page, source-image URL, creator credit, file hash, SKU, expected UPC, product expression, and selected size are recorded in `data/image-manifest.json`.

Open Food Facts documents its image license at https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/tutorials/license-be-on-the-legal-side/. License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/.

The Crown Royal Maple 750 mL photograph is by Jason White, sourced through Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons Attribution 2.0. Its source and original photograph URLs are recorded separately in the manifest. License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/. This license applies to that photograph; Open Food Facts images retain their own Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 terms.

These images remain available under that license. No project-specific restriction is added, and no generative editing, background replacement, or cropping has been applied. Each displayed file must be a clean, professional product packshot on a plain background, without people, hands, shelves or household surroundings. An exact product match alone is insufficient. Each reviewed file must depict the matching brand/expression, size and packaging without a visible price. An image for one SKU never establishes another size or sibling SKU's image.

Any Open Food Facts-derived metadata overlay retains source attribution and the applicable Open Database License; the store's independent inventory source is not an Open Food Facts export. Public product names and SKUs originate from the store's source inventory, with narrowly documented corrections. Bulk research data and unreviewed candidates remain private and are not shipped as verified product content.

The store-supplied Quick Stop logo is separate from these product photographs. This file does not grant rights to that logo or change its ownership.
